🧭 Pathfinding & Maze Visualizer

Escape 5 increasingly tricky mazes, then see how A* and Dijkstra would have done it.

How to play

  1. Choose a difficulty below and click Start Game.
  2. Move the blue player with ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D, or click a cell next to you.
  3. Reach the red goal to clear the level. Clear all 5 levels to win!
  4. Fewer moves earn more stars. Match the shortest path for ⭐⭐⭐.

Obstacles

  • Mud: you can wade through, but each step costs 3 moves.
  • ▲Spikes: step on them and you lose a ❤️ and go back to the start. Lose every heart and it's game over.
